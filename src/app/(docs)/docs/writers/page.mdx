The Dispatch is the society's publication at `/blog`. If your account has the writer
role, `/write` is your desk.

<Screenshot src="/docs/write-editor.webp" alt="The article editor with a title, cover image and body text" caption="The editor. Formatting appears when you select text; the insert menu appears on an empty line." />

## Starting an article

`/write` lists everything you have written with its current state. **New article**
creates an empty draft and drops you straight into the editor.

## The editor

| Part | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The headline. Also used for the browser tab and link previews. |
| **Subtitle** | One line under the title. Optional but worth writing. |
| **Cover image** | Uploaded from your machine. It is what appears on the Dispatch index and in link previews. |
| **Tags** | Free-form. They show under the article. |
| **Body** | The article itself. |

### Formatting

Select some text and a small toolbar appears over it: bold, italic, headings, links,
quotes. On an empty line, an insert menu offers images and other blocks. There is no
formatting toolbar pinned to the top, deliberately, so the writing surface stays
clear.

Images inside the body are uploaded the same way as the cover.

### Autosave

Your work saves automatically about a second and a half after you stop typing. There
is no save button and you do not need one.

<Callout type="note">
Autosave never changes an article's review state. Editing a published article does not
unpublish it, and editing a draft does not submit it. Only the submit button moves an
article forward.
</Callout>

### Reading time

Calculated from the body as you write and shown on the published article. It is not
something you set.

## Writing well for this publication

- Put the point in the first paragraph. Readers arrive from a link, not from the index.
- Use headings. They become the article's structure and help people scan.
- Give images captions and meaningful alt text.
- Check your links before you submit; the review queue will bounce broken ones back to you.
