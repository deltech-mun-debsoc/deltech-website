Nothing publishes itself. Every article goes through the secretariat's review queue.

## Submitting

<Screenshot src="/docs/blog-index.webp" alt="The public Dispatch index listing published articles" caption="Where an approved article ends up: the public Dispatch at /blog." />

Press **Submit for review** in the editor. The article moves from **Draft** to
**In review** and appears in the moderators' queue.

You can keep editing after submitting, but do not expect a reviewer to notice that you
changed something after they started reading.

## The five states

| State | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | Yours alone. Nobody else sees it. | Write. Submit when ready. |
| **In review** | Waiting on a moderator. | Wait. |
| **Changes needed** | A moderator wants edits. Their note is shown on your article in `/write`. | Make the edits, submit again. |
| **Published** | Live at `/blog`. | Nothing. Share it. |
| **Not published** | Rejected. The reason is shown with the article. | Read the note. A rewrite may be welcome; ask. |

## The review note

For **changes needed** and **not published**, the moderator writes a note explaining
why. It is shown inline against the article in your list at `/write`, and it is also
included in the email you receive. It is the most useful thing in the whole flow, so
read it properly before resubmitting.

## The emails

You get one when your article is approved, when changes are requested, and when it is
rejected. All three arrive at your account address.

## Resubmitting after changes

Edit the article and press submit again. It goes back to **In review** and returns to
the queue. There is no limit on how many rounds this takes.

## Who reviews

Staff at `/admin/blog`. See [moderating the Dispatch](/docs/staff/dispatch) if you want
to know what they see. Deleting an article is admin-only, so a rejected piece is not
destroyed; it simply stays unpublished.
