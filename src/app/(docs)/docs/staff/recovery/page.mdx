When something has gone wrong, work in this order. It is written to be followed under
pressure.

## The protocol

<Steps>
1. **Stop the blast radius.** If new submissions could make it worse,
   [close registration](/docs/staff/registration) first. You can reopen in ten minutes.
   You cannot un-create two hundred bad records.

2. **Read the activity log.** Go to [`/admin/logs`](/docs/staff/logs) and filter to the
   affected record. Establish who did what and when **before** deciding what to change.

3. **Prefer correction to deletion.** Edit the affected record. If the fix needs a
   guarded action, get an admin; do not work around it.

4. **Verify linked state.** One allotment touches the delegate, the allotment, the
   portfolio, the payment, the emails sent and the public matrix. Fixing one does not
   fix the rest.

5. **Recheck the public surface.** Look at the real public site: the registration state,
   the matrix, and the delegate's own status page. That is what your delegates see.
</Steps>

## Common situations

**Registration opened too early.** Close it. Look at what came in. The records are valid;
they just arrived before you were ready. Do not delete them.

**A committee was set up wrong.** Fix the committee. Then check anyone already allotted
into it: their fee was priced from the committee type at allotment and does not update.

**Payments were misrouted.** Close registration, get an admin to fix the provider, and
reconcile by hand what came in during the window. Do not comp people to make the problem
disappear.

**A delegate was allotted the wrong portfolio.** An admin revokes and re-allots. The
delegate has already had an email saying otherwise, so tell them.

**Bad rows were imported.** Check quarantine first: the rows you are worried about may
already be there. See [cross-delegation imports](/docs/staff/imports).

## Operator rules

These exist because each one has bitten somebody.

- Do not share admin or maintainer accounts.
- Do not use destructive actions as a shortcut for incorrect data.
- Do not mark payments received without external confirmation in hand.
- Do not open registration with incomplete committees, fees or payment routing.
- Treat the activity log as the source of truth for what happened.
- Escalate unclear financial, permission and deletion work to an admin.

## Afterwards

Write down what happened while it is fresh, including what the log showed. The next
person to hit it will be someone else, next year.
