Event control at `/admin/config` is the highest-leverage page in the console. It
decides what the public site is.

<Screenshot src="/docs/event-control.webp" alt="The event control page with mode selection and section switches" caption="Presets at the top, mode and event details in the middle, section switches below, with a live preview." />

## The three operating modes

| Mode | Homepage shows | Registration | Payments |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Society** | The society itself, no conference. | Structurally off. | Not applicable. |
| **Intra MUN** | The event. | Works normally. | **Entirely disabled.** |
| **Conference** | The event. | Works normally. | Normal paid flow. |

### What Intra MUN actually changes

This is the mode people misunderstand. In Intra MUN mode, allotment does not create a
payment at all. A delegate goes from **Registered** straight to **Confirmed** the
moment they are allotted, no payment link is emailed, no reminders are sent, and the
payment pages tell a delegate there is nothing to pay.

Use it for free internal events. Do not use it for a paid conference where you happen
not to have set the provider up yet, because delegates will be confirmed without
paying and there is no automatic way to reverse that in bulk.

## The four presets

Four one-click configurations that set the mode and the section switches together:

- **Society only.** Between conferences.
- **Free Intra MUN, open.** An internal event taking registrations.
- **Conference PR.** The conference is announced and visible, but intake is not open.
- **Paid registration.** The full live conference.

Start from the preset closest to what you want, then adjust. It is faster and less
error-prone than setting eight switches by hand.

## Event details

Name, label, subtitle, dates, venue and the call-to-action label. The live preview card
next to the form shows what the public hero will look like as you type.

## The eight section switches

Each turns one part of the public site on or off: active event, registration,
committees, matrix, Dispatch, team, quiz, recruitment.

<Callout type="note">
Turning a section off removes it from the homepage, the header **and** the footer at
the same time. You never end up with a nav link pointing at a hidden page.
</Callout>

## Two switches that are not sections

**Accept registrations** is the intake switch. See
[opening and closing registration](/docs/staff/registration).

**Payments** is admin-only. It is a global off switch for the whole payment system,
separate from the mode.

## Changing mode mid-conference

Possible, but think first. Moving from Conference to Intra MUN stops payments being
created for anyone allotted afterwards, while existing payments stay. Moving to Society
mode takes the conference off the public site while delegates still hold status links.
Neither is reversible by simply switching back, because the records created in between
already exist.
