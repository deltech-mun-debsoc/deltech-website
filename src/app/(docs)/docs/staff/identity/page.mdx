`/admin/site` holds the society's own words: who the society is, the awards it gives,
and how to reach you. The running event describes itself from
[Event control](/docs/staff/console), so its name, dates and venue are not here.

## What lives here

| Group | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Society copy** | The text used on the homepage when no conference is being promoted. |
| **Active event** | The current conference's public description. |
| **Venue** | Where it happens. Shown on the homepage and used on the payment page. |
| **Awards** | The list of awards given out. Rendered as its own homepage section. |
| **Query contacts** | Named people with an email or phone, for delegates who need to ask something. |
| **Email signatories** | Who outgoing emails are signed by. |

## Query contacts matter more than they look

They are the only route a delegate has to a human. They appear on the homepage and on
the payment page, and every troubleshooting path in the delegate docs ends with "ask
the secretariat". If they are empty or wrong, delegates have nowhere to go and the
secretariat's personal inboxes fill up instead.

Put at least one address there before opening registration, and use one somebody
actually monitors.

## Dates and venue

These also feed the launch checklist. If they are blank, the checklist keeps telling
you the conference is not ready, which is correct.

## Writing for the homepage

Keep it short. The homepage is a hero, a few sections and a call to action, not a
brochure. Long paragraphs wrap badly on a phone, which is where most delegates will
read it.

## Changing copy after launch

Safe at any time. Copy changes do not affect any delegate record, and the public site
picks them up immediately. This is not true of
[operating modes](/docs/staff/modes) or [fees](/docs/staff/fees), which do change what
happens to people.
