Staff accounts are created by invitation. There is no public route to one.

## Being invited

An admin adds your email at `/admin/users` and picks your role. You get a **staff
invite** email pointing at `/signin/staff`.

## The staff door

`/signin/staff` is the same sign-in mechanism as the delegate door, with the magic
link tab first, because a freshly invited account has no password yet.

<Steps>
1. Open `/signin/staff` and request a magic link with your invited address.

2. Click the link in your email. You are signed in and sent to your role's home page.

3. Go to `/account` and set a password, so you are not dependent on email every time.
</Steps>

## If the invite email never arrives

The invite creates your account regardless of whether the email is delivered. The
admin who invited you sees a warning when delivery fails and can simply send you the
sign-in URL by other means. You do not need to be re-invited.

## Rules

- **Do not share staff accounts.** The activity log records who did what, and a shared
  account makes it useless exactly when it matters.
- **Use your own address**, not a shared inbox, for the same reason.
- Your session is re-checked against the database periodically, so a role change or a
  disabled account takes effect quickly rather than at next sign-in.

## Leaving

Ask an admin to disable your account rather than deleting it. Disabling stops access
immediately and keeps your history in the activity log intact.
