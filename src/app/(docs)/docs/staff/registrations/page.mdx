`/admin/registrations` is the master list of everyone who has registered.

<Screenshot src="/docs/registrations.webp" alt="The registrations table with filters above it" caption="Filters across the top, twenty-five rows a page, click a row to open the drawer." />

## Finding people

A free-text search plus five filters: committee, status, source, DTU, and whether they
asked for accommodation. Columns are sortable. The list pages at twenty-five rows.

The filters are in the URL, so a filtered view can be sent to a colleague as a link.

## The delegate drawer

Clicking a row opens a drawer with everything about that person: their details, their
preferences, their allotment, their payment, their co-delegate, and the history of
every email sent to them.

### Editing

Editable from the drawer: full name, email, WhatsApp, alternative phone, institution,
DTU flag, MUN experience, both portfolio preferences, both accommodation switches, and
the reference.

<Callout type="warning">
Changing the **DTU flag** changes which fee tier applies. If the delegate is not yet
allotted this is free. If they are already allotted, their payment was priced at the
old tier and does not move; you will have to sort the difference out by hand.
</Callout>

Trying to change an email to one that already exists gives you a clear duplicate
message rather than a database error.

### Emails

The drawer lists every message sent to this delegate, with whether it succeeded. Any of
them can be resent from here. This is the fix for "I never got my allotment email".

## Exporting

Export as CSV or XLSX. **Filters apply to the export**, so set them before you press
it. Exporting the whole list when you wanted one committee is the single most common
mistake on this page.

Exports contain personal data. Treat the file accordingly: do not put it in a shared
drive without thinking, and delete it when you are done.

## What you cannot do here

Comping, cancelling, marking a payment received offline, and deleting are admin-only
and live in [payments and reconciliation](/docs/staff/payments). Allotment is its own
page: [the allotment floor](/docs/staff/allotment).

## Correcting rather than recreating

If someone registered twice, edit the good record and ask an admin to cancel the other.
Do not delete one and leave the other, and do not create a third.
