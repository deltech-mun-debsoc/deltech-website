Administrative Council only. `/admin/recruitment` creates cycles;
`/admin/recruitment/<cycle>` configures one.

## Creating a cycle

Two fields: a **name** between three and 120 characters, and a **slug** in lowercase with
hyphens, between three and 60. The cycle starts in **Draft**.

## Stage rules

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Group discussion required by default** | Whether new candidates need a GD. |
| **Interview required by default** | Whether they need a PI. |
| **Planned GD duration** | In seconds, from one minute to four hours. |
| **Planned PI duration** | Same range. |
| **Allow bypassing the group discussion** | Whether Senior Council can skip a candidate straight to interview. |

Durations are **planned**, not enforced. A session that runs over is not cut off; the
number sets expectations and drives the timer display.

## The two rubrics

One for group discussions, one for interviews. Each takes up to twelve criteria, and each
criterion has:

- a **key**: lowercase, starting with a letter, used internally;
- a **label**: what evaluators see, up to sixty characters;
- a **maximum**: the top of its scale, 1 to 100;
- a **weight**: 0 to 10, how much it counts relative to the others.

Criteria with different maxima are still comparable, because each is normalised before
weighting. See [scoring and the rubric](/docs/recruitment/scoring).

<Callout type="tip">
Fewer criteria score better. Twelve is the ceiling, not a target. Five or six well-chosen
ones produce more consistent scoring across evaluators than a long list that everyone
interprets differently.
</Callout>

## Selection email settings

A **WhatsApp group URL**, included in the email so selected candidates land somewhere,
and a **note** of up to a thousand characters added to the message.

Write the note before you need it. Sending selection emails is a moment when nobody wants
to be composing copy.

## Society roles

Fixed. Recruitment can only ever grant the plain member role. This is not configurable,
by design: recruitment cannot hand out console access. See
[finalisation and the society](/docs/recruitment/finalisation).

## Get it right before opening

Everything above can be changed later, but a rubric change mid-cycle means candidates
evaluated against different criteria. Configure in Draft, review, then open.
