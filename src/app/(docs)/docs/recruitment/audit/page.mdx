Recruitment keeps its own append-only record, separate from the admin activity log.

`/recruitment/audit`, for anyone with audit access, which includes Junior Council.

## What is recorded

Every action that changes a candidate, a session or a cycle: assignments, attendance,
session starts and stops, evaluations, stage moves, holds, bypasses, withdrawals,
disqualifications, result changes, imports and cycle transitions.

Each entry carries the event, the actor, the candidate where relevant, and the outcome.

## Filtering

By event, outcome, actor and candidate. Filtering by candidate gives you their whole
history in order, which is usually what you want.

## Append-only

Entries are not edited or deleted. A correction is a new entry, so the record shows both
what happened and what was done about it.

## Implicit authority is marked

When somebody acted using authority derived from their admin role rather than an explicit
council assignment, the entry records that. So "an admin did this because they are an
admin" is distinguishable from "a council member did this because they were assigned",
which matters when reviewing how a cycle was run.

## When to read it

**A candidate is in an unexpected state.** Filter to them and read forwards. The first
entry that is not what you expected is your answer.

**Two people disagree about what happened.** The log settles it.

**Reviewing a finished cycle.** Bypasses and overrides are worth reading as a group. A
cycle with many of either was run under pressure, and that is worth knowing before you
plan the next one.

## Do not share accounts

The log records the account, not the person. A shared login makes every entry say the same
thing and destroys the record's only purpose. This applies to council accounts exactly as
it applies to [staff accounts](/docs/staff/account).
