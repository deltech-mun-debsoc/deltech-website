Interviews are Senior Council and above. Junior Council members are redirected away from
this section entirely.

## The queue

`/recruitment/pi` lists **people**, not panels, in three groups:

- **Waiting**: candidates who have finished a group discussion, been bypassed past one, or
  never needed one;
- **In progress**: interviews happening now;
- **Completed**.

**Start interview** on a waiting candidate creates a one-person session and opens the
console.

## The console

Same session machinery as a group discussion: server-run timer, start, pause, resume,
finish, abort, and the controller lease.

The difference is what is on screen. The candidate's **group discussion verdict is shown
inline**, so you go in knowing what the GD panel thought rather than forming a first
impression the panel already formed.

Underneath that: the evaluation form for the interview rubric, and the post-interview
decision.

## After the interview

Submit your evaluation, then record the decision. The decision is a deliberate act; the
system does not infer it from the score. See
[decisions, holds and bypasses](/docs/recruitment/decisions).

## Reading the dossier first

Open [the candidate dossier](/docs/recruitment/dossier) before the interview. It carries
their verbatim application answers, their group discussion scores from every evaluator,
and their full stage history. Five minutes there makes a better interview than five more
minutes of it.

## Practical advice

- **Finish the session before the next candidate walks in.** A session left running keeps
  the candidate locked and skews the elapsed time.
- **Write remarks while it is fresh.** The remarks field takes four thousand characters
  and is what the Administrative Council reads at finalisation. "Good" helps nobody.
- **Use hold rather than guessing.** If you genuinely cannot decide, that is what hold is
  for.
