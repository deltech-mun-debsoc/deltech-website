`/recruitment/gd` holds group discussion panels. Junior Council and up.

<Screenshot src="/docs/gd-console.webp" alt="A live group discussion console with a running timer and evaluation forms" caption="A running session: server-run timer, the panel, and an evaluation form per candidate." />

## Creating a panel

The dialog offers candidates who still need a group discussion, so you cannot
accidentally schedule someone twice.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Names the panel. |
| **Scheduled time** | Optional. |
| **Candidates** | Up to sixty, though a workable GD is far smaller. |
| **Staff** | Up to twenty, each with a **may score** tick. |
| **Notes** | Up to two thousand characters. |

### The "may score" tick

This is the control that matters. A staff member on the panel without it can assist and
mark attendance but cannot submit an evaluation. Use it to bring Junior Council members
into panels for training without their scores counting.

## Running a session

Open the console for the group.

<Steps>
1. **Mark attendance** as candidates arrive. Absences are recorded, not left blank.

2. **Start** the session. The timer runs on the server, so every evaluator sees the same
   clock and reloading changes nothing.

3. **Pause and resume** as needed. Paused time is excluded from the elapsed total.

4. **Finish** when the discussion ends. Or **abort** if it collapsed and should not count.

5. **Submit evaluations** for each candidate, if you may score.
</Steps>

## Why you cannot double-book

A candidate can only be in one live session at a time. That is enforced at the database
level, not by the interface, so two Junior Council members creating overlapping panels
cannot produce a candidate expected in two rooms.

## The controller lease

One person drives the session controls at a time. This prevents two people pausing and
resuming against each other. If you need control, the console will tell you who has it.

## Aborting versus finishing

**Finish** means the discussion happened and evaluations count. **Abort** means it did
not: use it when a panel collapses, too few candidates turn up, or something went wrong.
Aborting is recorded with a reason.

Only Administrative Council can **reopen** a finished session, so finish deliberately.

## Your desk

`/recruitment` shows your own active and upcoming sessions with live timers, and the
evaluations you still owe. Start there each day rather than at the group list.
