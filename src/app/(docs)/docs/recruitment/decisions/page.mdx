A candidate's result is **stored because somebody set it**. It is never computed from
scores. This page covers every way it moves.

## The results

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | No decision yet. The default. |
| **On hold** | Deliberately undecided. Also what a tied panel produces. |
| **Selected** | In. |
| **Rejected** | Not in. |
| **Withdrawn** | The candidate pulled out. |
| **Disqualified** | Removed for cause. |

Withdrawn and disqualified are kept apart from rejected on purpose: they mean different
things and next year's council will want to know which.

## The stages

Intake, GD pending, GD active, GD complete or GD bypassed, PI pending, PI active, PI
complete, decision, closed.

Stage is where they are in the process. Result is what was decided. They move
independently.

## Advancing and holding

Junior Council and up can advance a candidate to the next stage, and hold one.

**Hold is not a soft rejection.** It means the decision is genuinely open. A tied panel
produces a hold automatically, and leaving it there until somebody looks properly is the
correct behaviour.

## Bypassing the group discussion

Senior Council and up, and only if the cycle allows it. It skips a candidate straight to
interview and **requires a reason**, which is recorded and shown permanently on their
dossier.

Reasonable uses: the candidate could not attend any GD slot, or they are already known to
the council. Unreasonable use: clearing a backlog. Every bypass is one candidate assessed
on less evidence than everyone else.

Only Administrative Council can **reverse** a bypass.

## Withdrawing and disqualifying

Both Senior Council and up. Withdraw when the candidate has told you they are out.
Disqualify for cause, and write down the cause.

## The Administrative Council powers

Reconsider, override and reverse a bypass. These exist to repair a cycle that has got into
a state it should not be in. Each one is recorded with who did it.

<Callout type="warning">
Override is a repair tool, not a shortcut. Using it to move a candidate whose evaluation
you disagree with hides the disagreement instead of recording it. Change the result, and
write the reason.
</Callout>

## Everything is recorded

Every one of these actions writes to [the audit trail](/docs/recruitment/audit) with the
actor, the candidate, the outcome and the reason where one was required.
