The form at `/register` has five steps. It only moves to the next step when the
current one is valid, so nothing is lost if you make a mistake.

<Screenshot src="/docs/register-step-1.webp" alt="Registration step one, asking for name, email, phone and institution" caption="Step 1 of 5. The step indicator at the top tracks where you are." />

## The fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Full name | Yes | At least two characters. This is what goes on your placard and certificate, so use the spelling you want printed. |
| Email | Yes | Everything is sent here: your status link, your allotment, your payment link and your receipt. It must be unique; one registration per address. |
| WhatsApp number | Yes | At least seven digits. Used for committee groups and urgent conference-day messages. |
| Alternative phone | No | A second number, useful if your WhatsApp number is not one you answer calls on. |
| DTU student | Switch | See below. |
| Institution | Yes | Your school or university. At least two characters. |
| MUN experience | No | Free text. A short note about conferences you have attended. It does not disqualify you if it is empty. |

## The DTU student switch

Turning it on locks the institution field to `Delhi Technological University` and
fills it in for you. Turn it off and the field becomes editable again.

This matters for more than tidiness: **fees are defined separately for DTU and
non-DTU delegates**, so this switch can change what you are charged. Set it
honestly. If you get it wrong, a staff member can correct it later and the fee is
recalculated at allotment, not at registration.

<Callout type="warning">
Use an email address you will still be able to read in a month. Changing it later
means asking the secretariat to edit your record, and any emails already sent to
the old address cannot be recalled.
</Callout>

## Common problems

**"This email is already registered."**
You, or someone using your address, has already submitted the form. Find your status
link in your inbox rather than registering again. If you genuinely cannot find it,
contact the secretariat.

**The institution field will not let me type.**
The DTU switch is on. Turn it off.

**Nothing happens when I press continue.**
A field above is invalid. The invalid field is highlighted and focused.
