Your status page lives at `/status/` followed by your private token. It is the single
place that always tells you where your application stands.

<Screenshot src="/docs/status.webp" alt="A delegate status page showing status, allotment and payment" caption="Everything on one page: status, allotment, payment and, once confirmed, the check-in QR code." />

## It needs no account

The link works for anyone who has it. That is convenient, and it is also why you
should treat it as private: your name, email, institution and payment state are all
visible on it.

If you lose the link, [create an account](/docs/delegates/account) with the same
email address and use [the dashboard](/docs/delegates/dashboard) instead.

## The statuses

| Status | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Registered** | Your form is in. Nothing is allotted yet. | Nothing. Wait. |
| **Allotted** | You have been given a committee and a portfolio. | Look at the allotment shown on the page. |
| **Payment sent** | A payment has been raised for you and a link emailed. | [Pay it](/docs/delegates/payment) before the deadline. |
| **Confirmed** | Paid, comped, or a free event. Your seat is secure. | Nothing until conference day. Your QR code appears. |
| **Waitlisted** | Registered, but not currently in line for a seat. | Wait, or contact the secretariat. This is set by hand, not automatically. |
| **Cancelled** | The registration has been withdrawn. | Contact the secretariat if this is unexpected. |

<Callout type="note">
At a free event there is no payment step at all. Allotment confirms you immediately
and you go straight from **Registered** to **Confirmed**.
</Callout>

## What appears when

- **Your allotment**, once you have one: committee, portfolio and the committee's agenda.
- **Your co-delegate**, if you registered as a pair.
- **Your payment**, once one exists: the amount, whether it is paid, and a Pay Now button.
- **Your check-in QR code**, only once you are `CONFIRMED`. It does not appear earlier.

## If you are signed in

The page offers a link to your dashboard. If you are not, it offers a sign-in prompt,
which is optional.

## Nothing is happening

Allotment is manual work and can take days, particularly right after registration
opens. Your status staying on **Registered** for a while is normal. Contact the
secretariat if a published allotment date has passed.
