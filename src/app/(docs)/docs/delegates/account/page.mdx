An account is optional. You can register, get allotted, pay and attend without ever
creating one, using only your private status link.

An account is useful because it gives you [a dashboard](/docs/delegates/dashboard)
that finds your registration by email, so you never have to keep track of the link.

## Creating one

Go to `/signup`. There are two tabs.

<Steps>
1. **Password.** Enter your email, choose a password of at least eight characters,
   and type it again to confirm. You are signed in straight away.

2. **Magic link.** Enter your email and nothing else. A sign-in link is emailed to
   you. Click it and you are signed in, with no password to remember. You can set a
   password later from `/account`.
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
Use the **same email address** you used on the registration form. The dashboard
matches your account to your registration by email address alone. It ignores
capitalisation, but it will not match two different addresses.
</Callout>

## If the address is already in use

Two things can cause this.

- **You already made a delegate account.** Sign in instead, at
  [`/signin`](/docs/delegates/signing-in).
- **The address belongs to a staff or writer account.** Signup deliberately refuses
  to convert an existing non-delegate account into a delegate one. Use a different
  address, or ask the secretariat.

## What an account does not do

Creating an account does not register you for the conference, does not reserve a
portfolio, and does not link an existing registration made under a different email.
Registration and accounts are separate things that happen to be matched by email.

## Rate limits

Signup is limited to five attempts per hour from the same address. If you hit it,
wait rather than retrying, or you will simply keep hitting it.
