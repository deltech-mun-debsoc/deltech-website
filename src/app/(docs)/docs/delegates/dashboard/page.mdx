`/dashboard` is the signed-in version of your status page. It exists so you do not
have to keep your private link.

<Screenshot src="/docs/dashboard.webp" alt="The delegate dashboard showing application status and allotment" caption="The dashboard finds your registration by matching your account email." />

## How it finds your registration

By email address, ignoring capitalisation. Your account email and the email on your
registration form must be **the same address**.

If they differ, the dashboard shows a "no registration found" card with a link to the
registration form. That does not mean your registration is missing; it means it is
filed under a different address. Two fixes:

- sign in with the account matching the address you registered with, or
- ask the secretariat to change the email on your delegate record.

## What it shows

The same things as [the status page](/docs/delegates/status): your status, your
allotment, your co-delegate, your payment and a Pay Now button when one is due.

## Only delegates land here

`/dashboard` is for delegate accounts. If you sign in with a staff, writer or council
account you are sent to that role's own home page instead. That is not a permissions
error, just a redirect. See [roles and permissions](/docs/staff/roles).

## Account settings

The header links to `/account`, where you can set or change your password, and to
sign out.
