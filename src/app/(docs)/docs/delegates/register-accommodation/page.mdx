Step 4 is two switches. Both are optional and both default to off.

| Switch | What it means |
|---|---|
| I need accommodation | You would like the secretariat to arrange a place to stay for the conference. |
| I am travelling from outside the NCR | You are coming from beyond the Delhi National Capital Region. |

## What these actually do

Neither switch books anything or charges you anything. They exist so the secretariat
can count how many people need housing before they arrange it. The admin overview
shows the running total of accommodation requests, and staff can filter the
registrations list by it.

Somebody will contact you separately about the details. Turning the switch on is a
request, not a confirmation.

<Callout type="note">
If your plans change after you register, ask the secretariat to update your record
rather than registering again. Staff can toggle both of these on your behalf from the
registrations desk.
</Callout>
