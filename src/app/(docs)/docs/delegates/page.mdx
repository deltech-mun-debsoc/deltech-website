## Before you start

You do **not** need an account to register. The form is public and takes five minutes.
Have ready:

- your full name as you want it printed on your placard and certificate;
- an email address you actually check, because everything important arrives there;
- a WhatsApp number;
- your institution name, unless you are a DTU student;
- if your first-choice committee is double delegation, your partner's name, email
  and phone.

It is worth opening [the portfolio matrix](/docs/delegates/matrix) in another tab
first so you can see which portfolios are still open before you name your
preferences.

## What happens after you submit

You are immediately given a private status link, and a confirmation email arrives.
Nothing else is required from you until the secretariat allots you a portfolio,
which can take days. Do not register twice while you wait; a second registration
with the same email is rejected.
