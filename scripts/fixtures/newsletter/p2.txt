The Bigger Picture

The Historic Summit

The United States, China and Russia are
the world's three largest nuclear powers
and permanent members of the UN
Security Council. Until this, their
leaders had never met together as a
trio.
The meeting came at a tense moment.
The US is at war with Iran, and attacks
in the Strait of Hormuz have pushed up
energy prices. Russia’s war in Ukraine
continues. Trump had said for weeks
that he wanted to discuss the war and
denuclearisation with both leaders, and
China, as this year's APEC host, brought
them together in Shenzhen.

THE ROAD TO SHENZHEN
FEBRUARY 2026: New START, the
last US–Russia nuclear arms pact,
expired.
NOVEMBER 2026: The leaders
confirm the first-ever three-way
meeting at Kylin Villa with a fourpoint joint declaration.

KEY DEVELOPMENTS
Nuclear Security: Dialogue to reduce
nuclear risks and prevent
proliferation; nuclear war “must never
be fought.”
UN Reform: Commitment to reform
the UN so it responds more effectively
to global challenges.
Energy Cooperation: Pledge to
increase production and supply of
reliable, affordable energy.
International Relations: Commitment
to maintaining a stronger, stable and
fair international security.

Global Impact
International politics: The talks reopen
top-level contact among the three powers:
Ukraine and its European allies, absent
from the table, have voiced concern about
being sidelined.
Energy markets: The full pledge targets a
market strained by attacks in the Strait of
Hormuz. No sudden or destabilising turns
were announced.
Nuclear security: Dialogue is a first step,
not a treaty. China’s far smaller arsenal
gives Beijing an incentive to accept limits,
while Trump seeks a new deal that
includes China.
Major-power relations: The declaration
also backed immunity for state officials and
warned the International Criminal Court,
which has a warrant out for Putin, against
abusing its jurisdiction.

Unresolved Challenges

The summit produced no settlement on the
Russia–Ukraine war or the Iran conflict. The
declaration did not mention either war, though
Trump said he was also on his own in one-onone talks with Putin. There was also no
undertaking to end the Ukraine war, and
renewed calls for talks with the US–China trade
truce due to expire by 10 January 2027.
