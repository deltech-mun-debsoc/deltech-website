CRICKET CHRONICLES

India beat Pakistan in a lastball U-19 Asia Cup thriller

India defeated defending champions Pakistan by one wicket in the final at Al Amerat, Oman,
chasing 338 and finishing at 339/9 in 50 overs. Mohit Ulva struck the winning boundary
from the final delivery to seal India’s record-extending ninth U-19 Asia Cup title.

How the match unfolded

India’s chase began with a 95-run opening stand.
Captain Lakshya Raichandani scored 80 off 101 balls
and Aaryavir Sehwag made 58 off 61. After the pair
departed, wickets fell in a cluster and India slipped to
252/8 in the 42nd over, still needing 86 runs.
Manal Chauhan (47 off 32) and Mohit Ulva (49 not out
off 26) revived the chase with a 57-run ninth-wicket
partnership. After Manal was dismissed at 309, No. 11
Chigurupati Venkata (11 not out off 8) joined Ulva.
India needed eight from the final over and three from
the last ball; Ulva bit Ahmed Hussain over long-off for
four, completing the chase.
